# Consecutive endpoint and supporting proofs, 2026-09-09

Fifteen of the twenty intended release targets are proved locally. The new
principal theorem gives the sharp uniform maximum and unique equality on
every m by (m+1) probability matrix for m>=19, and on its transpose. Zero
entries and arbitrary marginals remain included.

The [consecutive proof](../ENDPOINT_CONSECUTIVE_CUTS.md) combines 3,608 exact
positive non-whole cut inequalities for dimensions 19 through 29 with a
[factorial recurrence](../ENDPOINT_CONSECUTIVE_PARAMETERS.md) from 30 onward.
The finite inequalities are formula-defined rational Lean propositions,
without an external certificate evaluator. The actual matrix argument
constructs [minimum balanced dilation](../ENDPOINT-MINIMUM-DILATION.md),
retains the original zero and the forced complementary rectangle, and
applies the proved [permanent floor](../../data/ZERO_RECTANGLE_SOURCE.md).
Whole cuts and zero deficit are handled explicitly. Compact maximization
gives the result on the full closed simplex. Rejected controls include the
dimension-18 certificate and an incorrectly doubled finite-cut margin.

The same increment includes reusable long-column prerequisites. The
[leading foundation](../ENDPOINT-LEADING-FOUNDATION.md),
[stationarity identities](../ENDPOINT-LEADING-STATIONARITY.md),
[norm comparison](../ENDPOINT-LEADING-NORM.md) and
[saturated stationary bound](../ENDPOINT-SATURATED-STATIONARY.md) now give
[global saturated stability](../ENDPOINT-SATURATED-GAUGE.md) for every
probability board with m>=96. The compact minimum is taken on the actual
closed simplex; positive rows, the row cap and original kernel positivity
are derived. The rational penalty is continuous on its attained domain.

The [long-column kernel closure](../ENDPOINT-LONG-COLUMN-CLOSURE.md) derives
the retained kernel positivity and full uniqueness from two explicit
contender concentration bounds. It keeps the deleted-row avoidance factor
until its actual collision lower bound is applied. Those concentration
bounds and the remaining long-column principal theorems are still work to do.

The [square near-endpoint foundation](../../data/NEAR_ENDPOINT_FOUNDATION_SOURCE.md)
proves actual corner-zero padding, the exact rook/permanent identity and
shared marginal discrepancy; its [scalar tail](../../data/NEAR_ENDPOINT_PARAMETERS_SOURCE.md)
holds for every n>=26. The padding normalization is the boundary permanent
constant, not the uniform permanent constant. The independent two-zero
permanent gap and finite n=21 through 25 assembly remain pending.

Every included new production chain and its persistent boundary, equality
and rejected-hypothesis controls received independent semantic review.
Combined build and isolated source-package receipts are recorded in
[PROGRESS](../PROGRESS.md). The package excludes active downstream modules.
The prior fourteen-target replay remains in its
[historical record](2026-09-09-complete-low-orders-endpoints.md).
